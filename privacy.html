Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Personal Mail Tools is used only by its owner, with his own Google account.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, the tool reads, labels and drafts messages in the owner's Gmail and reads his Google Drive, Calendar and Contacts as needed to organize them.

Use and storage

Data is processed only on the owner's own computer, only for his own purposes. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Credentials are stored locally and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: the support email listed on the Google consent screen.
